// ABOUT PUMARESPONSE

After launching my vibecoding career with PumaSOC, I decided to see if I could make a companion game where instead of being the Operations lead, you played as one of the analysts.

The idea was simple - various incident scenarios with branching paths that gave you better or worse results. At higher levels, incidents would be related, requiring the player to recognize a pattern and infer a deeper issue that could then be tracked down in a threat hunt.

While developing this game, I started to realize I was actually building an incident-response mindset training simulator, as actual experienced handlers would easily know which of three static options to choose from.

I think there is something here that could be useful and/or entertaining for people wanting to get into incident handling, but I don't know anybody who ever wanted to get into forensics so they could be an MSSP analyst.

// HOW TO PLAY

You are an analyst at a Managed Security Service Provider. An incident lands in your queue, and you work it through six phases: Triage, Containment, Investigation, Eradication, Recovery and Post-Incident.

Each phase gives you a briefing, the IOCs, the evidence and a live SIEM feed. Tag the evidence lines that matter as findings: a real one can unlock a better response, and a false lead costs time. Then pick one of the responses. Your choices carry forward into later phases.

With the real-time clock, each phase opens with the clock stopped so you can read; once you start it, the attacker keeps moving until you decide. Turn-based play, in Settings, has no clock.

You are scored on response time, containment, evidence and thoroughness, and graded A to F against the best the scenario allows.

A–E or 1–5 pick a response · Enter starts the clock and carries on · M sound · Esc closes menus. In an incident, HELP in the header tours the screen.
